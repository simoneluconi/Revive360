# Screenshots

Screenshots referenced by the top-level [README](../../README.md). They are not taken yet: add
each file here under the name below.

Guidelines: PNG, portrait, taken on a real phone connected to a camera (not the simulator), light
or dark theme but the same for all six, status bar clean (no notifications). Crop nothing; the
README scales them. Avoid anything personal in the frame: no real network names other than the
camera's, no photos of people who did not agree to it. The camera's SSID and MAC appear on some
screens; blur them or use a camera you do not mind identifying.

| File | Screen | What it should show |
|---|---|---|
| `home.png` | Home | Connected camera with battery and storage, and the capture bar (photo / video / time-lapse / looping) |
| `settings.png` | Camera settings | The camera settings list, ideally with one option open |
| `liveview.png` | Live view | The live dual-fisheye preview from the camera |
| `gallery.png` | Gallery | The camera's files as thumbnails, photos and videos, ideally with multi-select active |
| `viewer360.png` | 360 viewer | A stitched photo in the 360 viewer, looking at an interesting part of the scene |
| `phone-gallery.png` | Phone gallery | The photos and videos already saved on the phone |
